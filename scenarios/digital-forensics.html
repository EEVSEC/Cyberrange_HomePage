Scenario · WAR

Digital Forensics Cyber Range Training

Every intrusion leaves a story in the artifacts — if you can read it before the attacker erases it.

ATK — Attacker mission

Move through the host and then cover your tracks: clear logs, timestomp, wipe artifacts, and break the timeline so the defender can’t reconstruct what you did.

DEF — Defender mission

Preserve volatile evidence, rebuild the attacker’s timeline from what’s left, attribute the actions, and prove what happened to a standard that would survive review.

AI — What the Coach reviews

Which artifacts you collected first, whether you spotted the anti-forensics, and how complete your reconstructed timeline is against ground truth.

MITRE ATT&CK techniques covered

  • T1070.001 Indicator Removal: Clear Windows Event Logs
  • T1070.006 Indicator Removal: Timestomp
  • T1027 Obfuscated Files or Information
  • T1485 Data Destruction
  • T1562.001 Impair Defenses: Disable or Modify Tools

Technique IDs are indicative of the kill chains modelled in this domain; each match draws a unique path through them.

Why this matters

  • NotPetya (2017) — a wiper disguised as ransomware that destroyed evidence as it spread.
  • Olympic Destroyer (2018) — deliberately planted false flags to mislead forensic attribution.
  • Routine log-clearing by intrusion sets to delay detection and frustrate response.
