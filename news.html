ISC2 · 2024

The cybersecurity workforce gap reached 4.7 million in 2024.

ISC2's annual study finds the global gap essentially unchanged — every open SOC role is an unfilled SOC role. This is the labour problem a cyber range exists to close: you build defenders by putting them in real fights, not slideware.

WAR Workforce gap

Cybersecurity Ventures · 2024

Cybercrime is projected to cost USD 10.5 trillion a year by 2025.

If cybercrime were a country, it would be the world's third-largest economy. The threat actors aren't slowing down — so the people who defend against them can't train on yesterday's static labs.

Cyber Crime Cost of crime

IBM · 2024

Breaches still take months to find — about 204 days of dwell time in India.

IBM's Cost of a Data Breach report shows attackers live inside networks for months before anyone notices. Detection speed is a trainable skill — and the only way to train it is under live pressure, which is exactly what a match measures.

WAR Detection & dwell time

Sophos · 2024

Around a third of GCC organisations were hit by ransomware in the last year.

The Gulf is a front line, not a sideshow. Sophos' State of Ransomware data underlines why regional teams — in the UAE and across the GCC — need range time against the exact playbooks targeting them now.

Cyber Crime Ransomware · GCC

Verizon · 2024

68% of breaches involved a non-malicious human element.

Verizon's DBIR keeps finding the same thing: most breaches turn on a human decision under pressure. You can't patch judgement — you have to rehearse it, which is what a live opponent forces you to do.
